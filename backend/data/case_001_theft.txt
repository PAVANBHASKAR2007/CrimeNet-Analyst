CASE REPORT
Case Number: FIR-2026-0112
Station: MG Road Police Station, Vijayawada
Date of Incident: 14 March 2026
Investigating Officer: Sub-Inspector Aditya University

SUMMARY:
On 14 March 2026, a complaint was filed by the owner of Sri Lakshmi Electronics, located near
MG Road, Vijayawada, regarding theft of electronic goods worth approximately Rs. 4,50,000.
CCTV footage from the shop shows two individuals entering the premises at approximately 1:40 AM.

WITNESS STATEMENT:
The night watchman, Ramesh Naidu, stated that he saw a man matching the description of
Ravi Kumar loitering near the shop around 11:00 PM on the night of the incident. Ravi Kumar
is known to frequent the Bhavani Function Hall area and was previously questioned in a similar
case at Governorpet.

SUSPECT DETAILS:
A mobile number 9876543210 was recovered from call records of a person seen near the location.
This number was traced to an individual named Ravi Kumar, residing in Governorpet, Vijayawada.
Bank transaction records show a deposit into account number 502012345678 shortly after the
incident, linked to Sunrise Traders, an organization believed to be a front for reselling stolen
electronics.

A vehicle bearing registration number AP16BM4521 was seen near the shop on CCTV around the time
of the incident.

REMARKS:
Investigators are requested to cross-check Ravi Kumar's involvement with any prior reports
involving Sunrise Traders or the same vehicle registration.
