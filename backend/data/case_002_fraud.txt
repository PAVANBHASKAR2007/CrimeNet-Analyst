CASE REPORT
Case Number: FIR-2026-0148
Station: Governorpet Police Station, Vijayawada
Date of Incident: 2 April 2026
Investigating Officer: Sub-Inspector Aditya University

SUMMARY:
A complaint of financial fraud was registered by Suresh Babu, owner of a small business in
Governorpet, alleging that he was cheated of Rs. 2,10,000 by individuals posing as agents of
Sunrise Traders, promising bulk supply of imported electronic goods at discounted rates.

DETAILS:
The complainant transferred money to account number 502012345678, believed to belong to
Sunrise Traders. After the transfer, the accused stopped responding to calls made to the number
9876543210, which the complainant had used to communicate with them.

Local sources identified one of the accused as Ravi Kumar, a resident of Governorpet who has
previously been questioned in connection with a theft case near MG Road. Another person
associated with the fraud, Suresh Chandra, was also named by the complainant as a co-accused.
Suresh Chandra is reportedly connected to Bhavani Function Hall, where the group is said to
have met to plan the scheme.

A vehicle with registration AP16BM4521 was reportedly used to transport goods shown to the
complainant as proof of stock.

REMARKS:
Given the overlap in mobile number, account number and vehicle registration with a prior case
(FIR-2026-0112), investigators should examine whether this is a coordinated criminal network
operating under the Sunrise Traders name.
